Standard Operating Procedure: Health Savings Account (HSA) Withdrawals and Penalties

General Withdrawals: Funds withdrawn from an HSA for qualified medical expenses are tax-free. If funds are withdrawn for non-qualified expenses before age 65, the amount is subject to standard income tax plus a 20% IRS penalty.

Fitness and Gym Memberships: Standard gym dues and fitness club memberships are universally classified as non-qualified expenses and will incur the 20% penalty. 

Exception (Letter of Medical Necessity): Gym membership expenses can only be reclassified as qualified medical expenses if the account holder submits a valid Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) from a licensed physician. The LMN must explicitly state that the facility usage is a prescribed treatment for a specific diagnosed medical condition (e.g., cardiovascular disease rehabilitation or obesity management). General health and wellness goals do not qualify for this exception.
